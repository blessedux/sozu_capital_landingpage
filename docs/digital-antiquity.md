# Digital Antiquity — Sozu Visual Canon

Canonical marketing and brand visual system for Sozu Capital.  
Domain terms: see [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md).

**Replaces:** Orb OS / design-system-v1 (deprecated archive).

---

## Core idea

Create an ethereal visual world combining monumental ancient architecture with primitive digital reconstruction.

The image exists somewhere between an ancient memory, a lost civilization, an early computer rendering, a scanned photograph, and a speculative future.

**Product frame:** this world expresses institutional trust for **Internet Money** — money on the internet you can trust. Mood frames; **Product Proof** (hold, send, Sozu Tag, cash-out) persuades.

**Style principle (central tension):**

| Stone | Data |
| --- | --- |
| Antiquity | Computation |
| Monument | Signal |
| Physical | Ethereal |

Architecture gives permanence. Fog removes certainty. ASCII reveals the world may be information.

Think of a civilization whose ruins are not being excavated but **decoded**.

## Subject language

Monumental **Doric** Greek architecture as the primary vocabulary: enormous stone pillars, fragmented colonnades, temple platforms, stairs, monolithic structures, incomplete ruins.

- Dramatically oversized and isolated in vast landscapes.
- Not a historically accurate reconstruction of Greece — an unknown civilization using classical language for mysterious infrastructure.
- Humans only sparingly: tiny, solitary silhouettes for scale.

**Avoid:** ornate Corinthian, fantasy-game temples, marble-statue collage, perfect architectural symmetry.

---

## Environment

Expansive, liminal environments:

- mountains disappearing into fog
- cloud-covered valleys
- empty deserts
- elevated plateaus
- impossible horizons
- structures emerging from mist
- architecture suspended above landscapes
- distant monumental silhouettes through atmosphere

Strong atmospheric perspective. Foreground dark and recognizable; distance increasingly abstract, luminous, dissolved.

Quiet, sacred, mysterious, slightly impossible. Never reveal the entire world — something always remains obscured by fog, darkness, distance, or digital dissolution.

## Digital reconstruction & Live Reconstruction

Parts of the physical world are reconstructed through primitive computational graphics.

ASCII, tiny circles, dots, raster marks, coordinate-like patterns, and point-cloud structures integrate **into** geometry — not as a flat overlay.

Characters and dots should:

- follow surface contours
- wrap around architecture
- describe depth
- follow landscape topology
- densify in shadows / structure
- dissolve toward fog and highlights
- occasionally replace parts of physical objects entirely

Some objects transition from photographic form into ASCII / point-cloud.

**Mark set:** `·` `.` `:` `+` `*` `°` `○` `◦` `⠂` `⠄` `⠆` `/` `|`

On the **website**, this layer is **Live Reconstruction** (DOM or canvas), bound by five laws:

1. **Surface-bound** — only on defined surfaces (hero architecture masks, stone silhouettes, fog bands); never full-viewport rain.
2. **Sparse** — texture at a glance; characters resolve only on close look.
3. **Limited marks** — mark set above (and kin); no words, hex dumps, or text-logos.
4. **Slow / scroll-linked** — reconstruction revealing, not a particle party.
5. **Hero-weighted** — ~90% of the effect lives in the hero; spine Product Proof stays mostly clear.

**Avoid:** ASCII rain, terminal gimmicks, random walls of text, RGB glitch, cyberpunk code aesthetics.

## Print texture

Treat finals as if they passed through several generations of physical and digital reproduction:

- coarsehalftone screens, visible printing dots
- offset-print texture, subtle paper grain, analog noise
- photocopy degradation, imperfect ink density
- fine raster lines
- slightly crushed shadows, softly blown highlights
- occasional imperfect registration

Visible up close; large forms readable at a distance. Printed, scanned, computational — not generic cinematic grain.

---

## Light

Diffuse, atmospheric, dreamlike. Large fog and bloom — not cinematic rim light.

Bright architecture or distant structures may glow softly through haze. Light may bleed slightly into neighboring colors (imperfect optic / print).

**Avoid:** glossy reflections, polished CGI materials, excessive lens flare.

---

## Composition

Monumental editorial compositions:

- enormous negative space
- distant horizons
- tiny human silhouettes
- partially obscured structures
- asymmetric placement
- extreme scale differences
- architecture disappearing outside the frame
- foreground silhouettes against luminous environments

The viewer should feel they have arrived somewhere much larger than themselves.

**Landing hero budget:** brand wordmark, H1 “Internet Money you can trust.”, subtitle “Hold, send, cash out. Built to grow, not erode.”, one CTA group, one dominant Digital Antiquity visual plane. No stats strips, floating badges, cards, or Sozu Tag mention on the hero.

## Color — Ethereal Palette (canonical web)

Default brand and landing chrome:

| Role | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Field / atmosphere | pale cyan, washed sky blue |
| Depth / fog | dusty lavender, fog violet |
| Structure / type | deep charcoal |
| Rare light accents only | faded coral, incandescent orange-pink (sun bloom — not a warm theme) |

Limited, print-oriented; large areas may read as duotone.

### Monumental Palette (rare)

Deep ultramarine, cobalt, warm ivory, bone white, aged paper, near-black — posters and rare deep compositions only, not default site chrome.

## Avoid (hard)

- Generic cyberpunk, neon futuristic city, glossy Web3 CGI, vaporwave
- Random meaningless walls of computer text, excessive RGB glitch
- Ornate Corinthian, fantasy-game temple aesthetic, marble-statue collage
- Perfect architectural symmetry, polished Unreal Engine look
- Generic cinematic concept art, excessive lens flare
- Conventional startup 3D illustration
- Orb as brand/landing centerpiece
- Atmosphere alone as the trust argument

The image should resemble a **forgotten institutional poster** documenting an impossible civilization through an imperfect computational imaging system.
